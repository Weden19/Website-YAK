Group rules

Not knowing the rules does not exempt you from responsibility. Please read them before joining.

Click a rule number to copy a link to it.

1. General provisions

  • 1.1 Not knowing the rules does not exempt you from responsibility.
  • 1.2 Taking part in the group means you agree to these rules.
  • 1.3 Advertising third-party projects, servers, websites and resources without the Administration’s permission is prohibited.
  • 1.4 The Administration consists of people with the “Organizational staff” role.
  • 1.5 The Administration may change the rules at any time without prior notice.
  • 1.6 The Administration may punish violators at its own discretion.
  • 1.7 The Administration may punish groups (companies of players) for the actions of their individual members in order to prevent systematic violations by the violator’s company.
  • 1.8 All decisions of the Administration are final and are reviewed only in exceptional cases.

2. Communication rules

  • 2.1 The languages of communication are Russian and English.
  • 2.2 Posting erotic, pornographic or excessively violent content is prohibited.
  • 2.3 Misleading others with malicious intent is prohibited.
  • 2.4 Posting or sending files with malware is prohibited if the malware was known about in advance.
  • 2.5 Disclosing a person’s personal information without their consent is prohibited.
  • 2.6 Promoting extremism and posting extremist materials is prohibited.
  • 2.7 Promoting or displaying Nazi (fascist) symbols is prohibited, including those of the “Azov” regiment, “Kraken”, the 3rd Assault Brigade, the RDK, the “Kastuś Kalinoŭski Regiment”, the LSR and similar.

3. Hierarchy

  • 3.1 Listen to people senior to you in position or rank.
  • 3.2 Follow the instructions of the instructors.

4. Behaviour at events

  • 4.1 Cadets’ aircraft are off-limits during training. Attacks on cadets will be stopped.
  • 4.2 Listen to the moderator.
  • 4.3 Using weapons against aircraft or helicopters that are taking off or landing, or that are parked with a pilot inside, is prohibited.
  • 4.4 Using weapons against the press helicopter or aircraft is prohibited (our photographer is inside).
  • 4.5 Before a dogfight starts, the other pilot must know that you want to fight. You can tell them by voice or by locking them with an air-to-air missile.
